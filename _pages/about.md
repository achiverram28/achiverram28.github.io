I am a first-year student in the two-year M.S. in Computer Science program at Yale University, advised by [Prof. Rex Ying](https://cs.stanford.edu/~rexy/). My research focuses on **geometric representation learning**, particularly **hyperbolic foundation models** and multimodal retrieval. 

Previously I interned at **Microsoft Research Lab – India**, building multimodal embedding models for document retrieval with [Vineeth N Balasubramanian](http://microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/vineethn/) and [Ravishankar Krishnaswamy](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/rakri/). I also collaborated on hyperbolic representation learning with [Prof. Menglin Yang](https://yangmenglinsite.github.io/). Prior to that, I was a Research Assistant and a Kotak AI undergraduate research fellow at [VISTA Lab](https://www.vistalabiisc.com/), **Indian Institute of Science**, working on large-scale data mining alongside graph and sheaf representation learning. I received my **Bachelor of Technology** in **Computer Science and Engineering** from Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Kottayam.
